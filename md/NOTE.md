# A Note: Knowledge, Ego and Order

These projects are the continuation of work I have been deeply invested in for years.
My CPU design is still in progress. Some of my methods exist only in my notebooks,
and for now they will stay there.

While organizing my scattered projects and writing summaries, I relied heavily on AI tools.
These tools had access to all of my files. I shared with them not only what I could express
in numbers, but also intuitions I could not put into numbers, ones I explained through
examples from the wider world.

I have witnessed the labor of people with valuable research being stolen. That is why I am uneasy.
Many people work on nearby questions. Someone may find these things before me, or after me.
From my smallest intuition to all the contributions of others who share their work and their valuable, brilliant insights, I do not want to stand by or enable these contributions to be presented as a non-human discovery while those people’s rightful credit for their work goes unrecognised.

I am in favor of sharing knowledge and what I know. These documents exist for that reason.
What troubles me is not sharing, but power used without thinking about the consequences.
In short, I oppose that kind of mission: giving everyone spoilers for films without even having watched them.

I have an ego too. I carry wounds from childhood. I have dreams and theories that people may
call crazy or naive before I can even get them to hear the end, and that can make even my social life harder. For thirty years, day and night, my mind has been occupied by these
thoughts, notes and ways of seeing. They form a structure shaped by the wounds in my mind.
Actually, I should say we have such a structure, because I am sure there are many people like me, some with extraordinarily brilliant minds. You might call me good. Of course there are people better than me, and better than you. I am probably better than someone too, but measured by what? Because I can read? Do not believe in what people call talent or a gift. Whoever is in the flow is always good. In Turkish I would call it “Allah vergisi.” I use the familiar expression without making a claim about anyone’s religion. Some minds are curious, and that is the real gift, not multiplying two 10-digit numbers in your head. Because, for me, a person who can multiply two 10-digit numbers is only someone who can multiply two 10-digit numbers. A human being is more than that. If we can help curious people bring out their potential, then we have succeeded too. The real satisfaction of the ego lies in sharing and improving.

Intuitions like these have meaning because of the children of those days. Children who were seen
as freaks, and who suffered socially, just because a butterfly's flight caught their attention
and they shared what they saw. Because of the knowledge those children built from their wounds,
gathered from the fields they were drawn to.
No one should ignore those people's labor and, without thinking about the consequences of their power,
post "look how well we can calculate".

We did not arrive at these intuitions through revelation. Other people's work lit the way for us,
and we walked by that light. What we need to know is this: we are part of the research,
not its source. This is a collective process.

When you invent a payment system, you can take out a patent.
But when it comes to knowledge, please do not start a chaotic, unplanned ego race that demotivates people.

James Augustus
October 10, 2026
